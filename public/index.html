            See bills in Apple Calendar

            Subscribe once and every bill with a due day shows up in Calendar (and in Parchment), with an alert the day before. It updates from here, so you never type a due date twice. On iPhone: Settings › Calendar › Accounts › Add Account › Other › Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the address. The phone has to be able to reach this computer.

            Tax rules

            Per income source ·

            Edit sources

            Employee: the employer withholds PR income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and you get a 499R-2/W-2PR. Services: the payer withholds 10% and sends you a 480.6SP. Nothing else is withheld, and you owe self-employment tax on your own. Uses the expected amounts from your income sources.

                What your forms should show

                compare with the real ones in January

                Rates checked October 2026. Current law: 0% up to $9,000, then 7%, 14% above $25,000, 25% above $41,500 and 33% above $61,500, with a $3,500 personal exemption. PS 912 (the governor's reform: 0% up to $12,500, then 6/12/24/29/33%) was still a bill in the Senate as of the latest sources found, so the default is current law. Not modeled: deductions, the earned income credit (crédito por trabajo), and SINOT/disability insurance (0.3% of the first $9,000 per employer, shown on pay stubs).
